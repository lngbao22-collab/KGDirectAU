# Loss Functions Reference

Mathematical definitions for eight link-prediction training objectives.

**Notation**

- $B$: batch size; $N$: number of entities (or negatives per row)
- $\mathcal{B}$: uniform distribution over batch rows; $\mathcal{E} = \{1,\ldots,N\}$
- $\mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}[\cdot]$: expectation over batch rows; $\mathbb{E}_{j \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}[\cdot]$: uniform over entities
- $s$, $\mathbf{s}$: model scores (higher is better unless noted)
- $y_i$: true entity index for row $i$; $\mathbf{t}_i \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^{N}$: multi-hot or smoothed label vector
- $\sigma(x) = 1/(1+e^{-x})$; $\mathrm{softplus}(x) = \log(1+e^{x})$
- $m$: margin; $\delta$: BCE logit offset; $\tau$, $\tau_{\mathrm{adv}}$: temperature scales; $\gamma$: additive margin on positives

---

## 1. Softmax family

### Cross-entropy

Logits $\mathbf{s}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$, true tail index $y_i$.

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CE}} = -\mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\left[\log \frac{\exp(s_{i,y_i})}{N \cdot \mathbb{E}_{j \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}[\exp(s_{ij})]}\right] = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\big[\mathrm{CE}(\mathbf{s}_i, y_i)\big]
$$

### KL divergence

Label-smoothed or multi-hot targets $\mathbf{t}_i$. Let

$$
p_{ij} = \frac{t_{ij}}{N \cdot \mathbb{E}_{k \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}[t_{ik}]}, \qquad q_{ij} = \mathrm{softmax}(\mathbf{s}_i)_j.
$$

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{KL}} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\big[\mathrm{KL}(\mathbf{p}_i \,\|\, \mathbf{q}_i)\big] = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\,\mathbb{E}_{j \sim \mathbf{p}_i}\big[\log p_{ij} - \log q_{ij}\big]
$$

With one-hot targets, $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{KL}} = \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CE}}$.

### InfoNCE

In-batch contrastive learning. Query vectors $\mathbf{q}_i$, candidate vectors $\mathbf{t}_j$, temperature $\tau$, optional margin $\gamma$ on positives:

$$
\ell_{ij} = \tau \cdot \mathbf{q}_i^{\top} \mathbf{t}_j, \qquad \ell_{ii} \leftarrow \ell_{ii} - \gamma.
$$

Diagonal labels $y_i = i$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{InfoNCE}} = -\mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\left[\log \frac{\exp(\ell_{ii})}{B \cdot \mathbb{E}_{j \sim \mathcal{B}}[\exp(\ell_{ij})]}\right] = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\big[\mathrm{CE}(\boldsymbol{\ell}_i, i)\big]
$$

## 2. BCE family

$$
\mathrm{BCE}(s, t) = \max(s, 0) - s \cdot t + \log\left(1 + e^{-|s|}\right), \quad t \in \{0, 1\}
$$

Equivalently: $\mathrm{BCE}(s, t) = -\left[t\log\sigma(s) + (1-t)\log(1-\sigma(s))\right]$.

### Broadcast BCE

Scores $\mathbf{s}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$, targets $\mathbf{t}_i$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BCE}}^{\mathrm{broadcast}} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B},\, j \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}\big[\mathrm{BCE}(s_{ij} + \delta,\; t_{ij})\big]
$$

### Negative-sampling BCE

Per row: positive score $s^{+}$, negative scores $\{s^{-}_{k}\}_{k=1}^{N}$.

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{row}} = \mathrm{BCE}(s^{+} + \delta, 1) + N \cdot \mathbb{E}_{k \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}\big[\mathrm{BCE}(s^{-}_{k} + \delta, 0)\big]
$$

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BCE}}^{\mathrm{negsamp}} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\big[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{row}}^{(i)}\big]
$$

Equivalently:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{row}} = -\log\sigma(s^{+} + \delta) - N \cdot \mathbb{E}_{k \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}\big[\log\sigma\left(-(s^{-}_{k} + \delta)\right)\big].
$$

### Adversarial BCE

Self-adversarial negative weights $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_i$, subsampling weights $w_i$:

$$
\alpha_{ik} = \mathrm{softmax}\big(\tau_{\mathrm{adv}}\, \mathbf{s}^{-}_{i}\big)_k = \frac{\exp\left(\tau_{\mathrm{adv}}\, s^{-}_{ik}\right)}{N \cdot \mathbb{E}_{k' \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}\big[\exp\left(\tau_{\mathrm{adv}}\, s^{-}_{ik'}\right)\big]}
$$

$$
\mathcal{L}^{+}_{i} = -\log\sigma(s^{+}_{i}), \qquad \mathcal{L}^{-}_{i} = -\mathbb{E}_{k \sim \boldsymbol{\alpha}_i}\big[\log\sigma\left(-s^{-}_{ik}\right)\big]
$$

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{adv\text{-}BCE}} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\!\left[\frac{\mathcal{L}^{+}_{i} + \mathcal{L}^{-}_{i}}{2};\; w_i\right]
$$

where $\mathbb{E}[f_i;\, w_i] = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim w_i / \mathbb{E}_j[w_j]}[f_i]$ is the weighted batch expectation.

## 3. BPR (Bayesian Personalized Ranking)

Pairwise ranking loss (negative-sampling only). Per positive–negative pair:

$$
\ell(s^{+}, s^{-}) = -\log\sigma(s^{+} - s^{-}) = \mathrm{softplus}(s^{-} - s^{+})
$$

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{BPR}} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B},\, k \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E})}\big[\mathrm{softplus}(s^{-}_{ik} - s^{+}_{i})\big]
$$

Weighted variant: $\mathbb{E}[\ell;\, w_i]$ over batch rows with subsampling weights $w_i$.

Contrast with negsamp BCE, which scores positives and negatives independently rather than ranking $s^{+}$ above $s^{-}$.

---

## 4. Margin ranking

Pairwise hinge (higher scores are better):

$$
\ell(s^{+}, s^{-}) = \max\left(0,\; m - s^{+} + s^{-}\right) = \mathrm{ReLU}(m - s^{+} + s^{-})
$$

### Negative-sampling margin

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{margin}}^{\mathrm{pair}} = \mathbb{E}_{(i,k) \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{P})}\big[\max\left(0,\; m - s^{+}_{i} + s^{-}_{ik}\right)\big]
$$

where $\mathcal{P}$ is the set of all positive–negative pairs in the batch.

### Broadcast margin (1vsAll)

For row $i$ with true entity $y_i$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{row}} = \mathbb{E}_{j \sim \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{E} \setminus \{y_i\})}\big[\max\left(0,\; m - s_{i,y_i} + s_{ij}\right)\big], \qquad \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{margin}}^{\mathrm{1vsAll}} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\big[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{row}}^{(i)}\big]
$$

### Broadcast margin (KvsAll)

For each row, positive set $\mathcal{P}$ and negative set $\mathcal{N}$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{row}} = \mathbb{E}_{j \sim \mathbf{p}^{+}_i,\; k \sim \mathbf{p}^{-}_i}\big[\max\left(0,\; m - s_{j} + s_{k}\right)\big]
$$

where $\mathbf{p}^{+}_i$ and $\mathbf{p}^{-}_i$ are uniform over $\mathcal{P}$ and $\mathcal{N}$.

---

## 5. Pointwise logistic (DaBR)

Plausibility scores (higher is better). Label $Y \in \{+1, -1\}$:

$$
\ell(s, Y) = \mathrm{softplus}(-Y \cdot s) = \begin{cases} \mathrm{softplus}(-s) & Y = +1 \\ \mathrm{softplus}(s) & Y = -1 \end{cases}
$$

Negative-sampling objective over flattened positive and negative pairs:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DaBR}} = \mathbb{E}_{(s, Y) \sim \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{pt}}}\big[\mathrm{softplus}(-Y \cdot s)\big]
$$

where $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{pt}}$ is uniform over all $B(N{+}1)$ score–label pairs in the batch.

Weighted variant:

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DaBR}}^{w} = \mathbb{E}_{i \sim \mathcal{B}}\!\left[\frac{\mathcal{L}^{+}_{i} + \mathbb{E}_{k}[\ell(s^{-}_{ik}, -1)]}{2};\; w_i\right].
$$

## Paradigm summary

Learning-to-rank paradigms classify objectives by the **scoring unit** each term optimizes:

- **Pointwise** — independent score–label pairs; no direct comparison between candidates.
- **Pairwise** — each term ranks a positive above a negative via a margin or logit difference.
- **Listwise** — each term normalizes or matches a distribution over an entire candidate list (softmax / KL).

| BPR | NegSamp | Pairwise |

| Pointwise logistic (DaBR) | NegSamp | Pointwise |
